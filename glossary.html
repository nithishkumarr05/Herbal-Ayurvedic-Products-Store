Ingredients Glossary

Discover the healing properties of Ayurveda's most revered herbs — each one a gift from nature.

Ashwagandha

A powerful adaptogen, Ashwagandha helps the body manage stress, supports vitality, and promotes restful sleep. Traditionally used to strengthen the nervous system and enhance endurance.

Energy Stress Relief Sleep

Tulsi

Known as the "Queen of Herbs," Tulsi is revered for its immune-supporting and adaptogenic properties. It helps the body adapt to physical and emotional stress while promoting respiratory health.

Immunity Respiratory Stress

Brahmi

A renowned nootropic, Brahmi enhances cognitive function, improves memory, and calms the mind. It is traditionally used to support learning and mental clarity.

Cognition Calming Memory

Triphala

A classic Ayurvedic formulation of Amalaki, Bibhitaki, and Haritaki. Triphala is a gentle digestive tonic, supports detoxification, and promotes healthy elimination.

Digestion Detox Cleanse

Neem

Neem is a powerful purifier known for its antibacterial and antifungal properties. It supports clear skin, oral health, and overall immune function.

Purify Skin Health Immunity

Amla

Indian gooseberry, or Amla, is one of the richest sources of Vitamin C. It is a powerful antioxidant that supports immunity, hair health, and glowing skin.

Vitamin C Immunity Glowing Skin

Shilajit

A potent mineral-rich resin from the Himalayas, Shilajit is revered for its rejuvenating properties. It supports energy, stamina, and overall vitality.

Energy Vitality Stamina

Ginger

A warming spice used for centuries in Ayurveda, Ginger supports digestion, reduces inflammation, and helps relieve nausea. It's a staple for digestive health.

Digestion Inflammation Nausea Relief

Ancient Principles

Understanding Herbal Synergy

Key timeless concepts for unlocking the maximum potency of your Ayurvedic regimen.

Anupana (Carriers)

The vehicle used to consume herbs (such as warm milk, honey, or ghee) enhances absorption into target tissues and balances potency.

Rasa & Virya

Each plant possesses specific energetic qualities (sweet, bitter, heating, or cooling) designed to harmonize your primary Dosha structure.

Rutucharya Alignment

Adjusting herbal consumption in harmony with seasonal shifts protects immunity and maintains year-round equilibrium.
